## Summary

Extraction is governed by grind size, dose, temperature, pressure and time, but
these are not independent — changing grind changes effective contact time, so
you cannot hold one variable and sweep another cleanly. The practical method is
to fix everything except grind and move in one direction until the shot flips
from sour to bitter, then back off.

## Notes
- You cannot taste a shot without destroying it. Every measurement consumes the sample.
- Feedback is immediate here (~30s) which is why espresso is learnable at all.
- Contrast with processes where feedback takes days — the same search strategy
  becomes impractical.
- Logging matters more than intuition: without a written record you re-walk the
  same part of the space repeatedly.
