## Summary

Progressive disclosure keeps an interface focused on the decision a person is
making now, while leaving less common or advanced controls available when they
become relevant. It lowers the effort of getting started without treating
expert needs as expendable.

## Notes

- *No captured highlight — this note predates highlight capture.*
  - *A useful default path can be simple even when the underlying system is not.*
  - *Reveal additional controls in response to a clear need, rather than making every person parse every possibility up front.*
