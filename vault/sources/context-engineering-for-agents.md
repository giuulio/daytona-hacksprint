## Summary

Once models are commoditised, the differentiator is what you put in front of
them. Retrieval quality, tool design and the shape of the working environment
dominate phrasing.

## Notes
- *No captured highlight — this note predates highlight capture.*
  - *Giving an agent a real shell and a real filesystem outperforms giving it a larger prompt, because it can look things up rather than be told everything.*
  - *The scarce resource is relevant context, not tokens.*
