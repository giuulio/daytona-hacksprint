## Summary

Ambient music is designed to reward attention without demanding it. Eno's
generative pieces specify rules and let phase relationships between loops of
incommensurate length produce non-repeating output from very little material.

## Notes
- The composer specifies a process, not an artifact.
- Ambient work is explicitly designed *for a state* — a time of day, a level of
  attention — which is unusual among media.
