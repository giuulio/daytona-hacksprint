## Summary

Jensen Huang describes AI investment as a reinforcing loop: additional compute
improves AI capabilities, which expands use, produces revenue, and funds the
next round of compute. The useful question is where that loop slows or breaks,
rather than assuming each link will keep scaling smoothly.

## Notes

- *The limiting factor may move between hardware supply, model quality, useful applications, adoption, willingness to pay, and capital availability.*
- *More capability does not automatically translate into durable usage or revenue; each handoff in the loop needs evidence.*
