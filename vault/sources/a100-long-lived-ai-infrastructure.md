## Summary

Jensen Huang characterizes NVIDIA's Ampere-based A100, introduced in 2020, as hardware that is still commercially useful six years later across training, fine-tuning, inference, and high-performance computing. The claim is a useful reminder that AI infrastructure can have a much longer working life than the rapid pace of model releases suggests.

## Notes

- *A single accelerator generation can serve several workloads over time, rather than being tied to its original training use case.*
- *This is a vendor statement, so it is evidence of NVIDIA's positioning as much as an independently verified market measure.*
