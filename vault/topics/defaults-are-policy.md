title: Defaults are policy
tags: [behavioural-economics, design, systems, choice]
status: evergreen
created: 2026-01-12
---

Whatever the system does when nobody chooses is the choice almost everyone gets.
Organ donation opt-in vs opt-out is the standard example; the preference does not
change, the default does, and the outcome swings by tens of percent.

Consequence for software: the default is the product for most users. Configuration
is a feature for the minority who deviate — and the deviations are worth reading.
