Keeping the original alongside the cleaned-up version. Archives, scientific data
and version control all converge on the same rule: never destroy the raw record,
because every act of tidying encodes an interpretation you may later want to
revisit.

Applies directly to note-taking. A summary written six months ago is a claim about
the source, not the source.
