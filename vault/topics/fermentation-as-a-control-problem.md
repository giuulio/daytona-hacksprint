Sourdough, koji, cheese, kimchi: in all of them you set initial conditions and
then wait, and the only lever during the run is temperature. The measurement
problem defines the craft — you cannot sample without destroying, and the result
arrives long after the decision.

This shape recurs far outside food: anything with delayed, destructive feedback
where the practitioner keeps a log they never manage to analyse.
