Dark mode is the only concession most software makes to the fact that a human at
03:00 is a different sensory instrument than the same human at 15:00, and it is
triggered by a preference toggle rather than by anything about the reader.

If contrast sensitivity genuinely drops at circadian trough, then type size,
weight, contrast and motion are all candidates for adaptation, not just palette.
