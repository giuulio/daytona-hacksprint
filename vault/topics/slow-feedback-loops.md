Skill acquisition is roughly a function of feedback latency. Chess and espresso
are learnable because the loop closes in seconds. Training blocks, fermentation,
career decisions and macroeconomics are hard because the loop closes in weeks or
years, by which time a dozen other variables moved.

The compensating move is always the same: log obsessively at decision time,
because you will not remember the inputs when the output finally lands. Almost
nobody closes the loop back to the log.
